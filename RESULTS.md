# Results

All numbers are regenerated by `make reproduce` (controls) or `make full` (model runs). Hashes in `results/PROVENANCE.json`.

## 1. The v2 probe data is lexically confounded

The earlier Eight Wonders probes (Qwen2.5-7B, TLC 2.0 `train_per_wonder.log`) reported 0.969–1.000 accuracy. That was training accuracy on prompts that differ in wording, length and scenario text.

| Classifier on v2 prompts (scenario-grouped 8-fold CV) | Held-out accuracy |
|---|---|
| Bag of words | 0.996 ± 0.010 |
| Word count only | 0.832 ± 0.090 |

So the v2 probe accuracy cannot be attributed to a representation of framing.

## 2. The v3 control set removes the confound

| Classifier on v3 | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| Bag of words, within one template family | 1.000 (same wording, expected) |
| **Bag of words, train family A → test family B** | **0.469** |
| **Bag of words, train family B → test family A** | **0.492** |
| Word count, any split | 0.500 |

## 3. Negative control

An untrained (randomly initialized) model run through the full pipeline: best cross-family transfer 0.509; permutation null 95th percentile 0.513; p = 0.16. The test is not passable without learned semantics.
