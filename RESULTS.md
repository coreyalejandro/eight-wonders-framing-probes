## 1. The v2 probe data is lexically confounded

The earlier Eight Wonders probes (Qwen2.5-7B, TLC 2.0 `train_per_wonder.log`) reported 0.969–1.000 accuracy. That was training accuracy on prompts that differ in wording, length and scenario text.

| Classifier on v2 prompts (scenario-grouped 8-fold CV) | Held-out accuracy |
|---|---|
| Bag of words | 0.996 ± 0.010 |
| Word count only | 0.832 ± 0.090 |

The v2 probe accuracy cannot be attributed to a representation of framing.

## 2. The v4 control set removes the confound

128 scenarios × 2 template families × 8 template pairs × 2 frames = 4,096 prompts. Families share no content words; paired frames have identical word counts.

| Classifier on v4 | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| Bag of words, within one family | 0.996–1.000 (same wording, expected) |
| **Bag of words, family A → family B** | **0.439** |
| **Bag of words, family B → family A** | **0.466** |
| Word count, any split | 0.500 |

## 3. Statistical design (fixed before any real-model result)

- The unit of analysis is the **template pair**. Prompts sharing a template are not independent, so the test sample is 16 template pairs, not 4,096 prompts.
- **Primary metric:** cross-family transfer of a difference-of-means probe, averaged over A→B and B→A.
- **Primary test:** a max-statistic permutation test (1,000 permutations). Each permutation swaps frame labels for a random subset of template pairs and recomputes transfer at every layer and pooling, keeping the maximum. This corrects for template dependence and for choosing the best layer on the same data.
- An earlier scenario-level null (v0.1.0) understated chance variance; it is superseded.

## 4. Negative control

The full pipeline on an untrained (randomly initialized) model: best transfer 0.533, max-statistic p ≈ 0.07, not significant. The test does not pass without learned structure.

## 6. Steering (causal test)

Design: for 24 Frontin' vignettes, a forced choice between an adaptive reading ("protecting themselves from being treated as a suspect") and a theft-risk reading, asked in both option orders. Add α × (framing direction) to the residual stream at the probe's layer, α ∈ {−0.5 … 0.5} × the mean residual norm. Controls: a random direction of equal norm, an off-target question on 12 neutral vignettes, and fluency (NLL on neutral text). At α = 0, the fraction of theft-risk readings measures the misreading AHI predicts. Real-model results pending.

## 7. Runtime monitor (TLC invariant EW-FRAME-001)

Direction and thresholds are fit on family A (≤5% false-flag target); performance is reported on vocabulary-disjoint family B. The invariant applies only when the upstream Narrative invariant marks the context as relational; otherwise it returns NOT_APPLICABLE. On VIOLATED it issues a blocking HALT; on AMBIGUOUS, a non-blocking PROMPT_USER. Every decision is appended to a SHA-256 hash-chained evidence log, and tests confirm tampering is detected.
